TYPE 01

2D / 3D Hologram Master

Our 2D/3D holographic technology creates a striking sense of physical depth by layering multiple two-dimensional images directly behind one another. Because each layer sits at a different distance from the viewer's eyes, the result is a realistic 3D visual effect complete with natural parallax. Using ultra-fine diffractive lines, these holograms remain sharp, vivid, and visible even at wide viewing angles. We can turn virtually any logo or design into a multi-layered hologram. Using specialized design software, our team converts your existing graphics into color-separated visual layers—typically combining 2 to 4 depth layers and up to 6 colors.
